FAQ

Quick answers before you book

We keep it straightforward. If your landlord/housing office has a checklist, send it — we’ll align to it.

Is carpet cleaning included?

Yes. Standard carpet extraction is included in every package. Severe staining, pet damage, or replacement decisions are landlord-controlled and may still be charged by the property.

What is the “documentation package”?

A photo set of key rooms + common problem areas and your invoice for records. It’s designed to support your move-out condition story and reduce confusion later.

Do you guarantee I’ll get my deposit back?

No. We cannot guarantee deposit refunds or inspection outcomes because landlords and lease terms vary. We provide inspection-focused cleaning + documentation to help reduce avoidable deductions.

What add-ons do you offer?

Only three: trash removal, wall hole touch-up (small nail/screw holes), and odor treatment. Everything else is outside scope unless explicitly agreed in writing.

Do you do repairs, painting, or major patchwork?

We are primarily a cleaning service. We only do small wall hole touch-up as an add-on. Full repainting, major repairs, or specialty remediation are not included.

How do I get a quote fast?

Text (580) 215-4212 with: home type, bedrooms, pets, move-out date, and city/address area. Mention any add-ons (trash/touch-up/odor) if needed.

Are prices final?

Prices listed are base package pricing + tax. Final pricing can vary for extreme conditions (heavy pet hair, excessive trash, restricted access, biohazards/unsafe conditions). We confirm scope before starting.

What areas do you serve?

Lawton & Fort Sill area and nearby communities. If you’re outside the typical area, text us and we’ll confirm availability.
