Why this saves people money

Deposits disappear fast because move-out deductions stack up. A common example:

  • “Deep clean” deduction: $250–$450
  • Carpet “treatment” or cleaning: $150–$300
  • Trash-out / haul-away: $75–$200
  • Wall patch/paint touchups: $75–$250

That can wipe out a $800–$1,500 deposit quickly. Deposit Defense is designed to reduce avoidable charges by delivering an inspection-focused clean plus documentation you can keep.

FAQ

Clear expectations • Lease-style scope • Disclaimers included
Does this guarantee I’ll get my deposit back?

No. We provide cleaning and documentation services, not legal advice. Deposit outcomes depend on the lease, the property’s condition, and the landlord’s policies. Our goal is to reduce avoidable deductions and help you document move-out condition.

Is carpet cleaning included?

Yes — standard carpet extraction is included in every order. Severe staining, damage, or replacement decisions are landlord-controlled and may still be charged by the property owner.

What exactly is the “documentation package”?

A photo set of key rooms and common deduction areas (plus any problem areas we identify) and an invoice for your records. We may also reference your move-out checklist if you provide one.

What add-ons do you offer?

Only these: trash removal, wall hole touch-up (small patch + quick touch-up), and odor treatment. Everything else is included in the base service.

What’s not included?

Major repairs, repainting, mold remediation, hazardous waste removal, pest treatment, biohazards, and specialized remediation work. If something needs a specialist, we’ll tell you up front.

Are prices final?

Prices shown are starting points and are + tax. Final price depends on size, condition, access, and any approved add-ons. We confirm scope before work begins.

Do you follow Fort Sill / on-post housing checklists?

If you have a checklist from on-post housing or your landlord, send it — we’ll use it as a reference. Final acceptance still depends on the housing office/landlord.
