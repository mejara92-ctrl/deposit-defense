FAQ

Clear expectations = fewer surprises

Do you guarantee I’ll get my deposit back?

No. We can’t control lease terms, property condition, or landlord decisions. What we do is deliver a lease-style, inspection-focused clean and provide documentation you can keep. Many customers want this because it reduces ambiguity and helps avoid “he said / she said.”

Is carpet cleaning included?

Yes — standard carpet extraction is included in every package. Landlords may still charge for severe staining, odor, damage, or replacement decisions outside our control.

What exactly is the documentation package?

A photo set of key rooms + common deduction areas (examples: kitchen, bathrooms, floors/baseboards, and visible condition items), plus a paid invoice for your records. We provide documentation for your file — it is not legal advice.

What add-ons do you offer?

Only three: trash removal, wall hole touch-up, and odor treatment. Everything else is outside scope unless it’s part of the standard move-out cleaning and carpet extraction.

What if my landlord has a checklist?

Perfect — send it to us. We’ll use it as a reference to align the cleaning focus with what your property actually checks during move-out.

How fast can I book?

Texting is fastest. Send your home type (apartment/small home/large home), bedrooms, move-out date, and any add-ons. We’ll confirm scope and scheduling by text or phone.

Are prices final?

Prices shown are + tax. Final pricing can vary for extreme conditions (heavy pet hair, excessive trash, biohazards, hazardous materials), restricted access, or non-standard scope. If something is out of scope, we’ll tell you before starting.

Do you work on Fort Sill?

We serve Lawton & Fort Sill area. Access rules can vary by location. If you’re on-post, text us the address/area and we’ll confirm access requirements.
