FAQ

Quick Answers.

Will this get my full deposit back?

We can't promise that. Landlords decide based on the lease, the home's condition and their own policies, and they can charge for damage or wear we can't clean away. What we can do is leave the home inspection-ready and give you photos that show how you left it.

Is the online price final?

It's the price for a typical move-out. Your crew does a quick walkthrough on arrival and confirms the price with you before starting. If something like heavy buildup or furniture left behind changes the job, you'll hear it before any work begins.

What's in the photo documentation?

Time-stamped photos of each room and the spots inspectors usually check, plus your invoice, sent to the email you book with.

Is carpet cleaning included?

Carpet cleaning is $50 per room, added when you book. Pet enzyme treatment is $25 per room. Heavy stains and damage can't always come out, and replacement is the landlord's call.

Does the home need to be empty?

It works best empty, with water and power on. We clean around anything left behind, and the crew will confirm the plan on arrival.

When do I pay?

After the clean, once you've seen the work. Prices are + tax.

What don't you handle?

Mold remediation, biohazards, hazardous materials, hoarding situations and repairs beyond small wall-hole touch-ups. We'll tell you up front if a job needs a specialist.

Where do you work?
