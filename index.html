FAQ

Quick answers

How fast can you schedule?

Depends on the week. Call/text and we’ll give you the earliest openings immediately.

Do you guarantee I’ll get my deposit back?

No one can guarantee what a landlord will do — but we clean to inspection items and provide documentation so you’re in the strongest possible position.

What do you need from me before the clean?

Ideally the place is empty (or almost empty). Utilities on is helpful. If there’s heavy trash or items left behind, ask about trash-out.

Is this “deep clean” or “move-out clean”?

This is move-out focused: kitchens/baths + detail items that usually trigger deductions (baseboards, edges, touch points). We’ll confirm scope on the phone.
