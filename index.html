FAQ

Questions renters ask before move-out

Short answers. No fluff. If you want a quote, call or text (580) 215-4212.

Do you guarantee I’ll get my security deposit back?

No. We can’t control landlord decisions, lease clauses, or pre-existing condition. What we do is deliver an inspection-focused clean and provide documentation for your records, which may help reduce surprise deductions.

What does “documentation included” mean?

We provide a photo set of key rooms and common problem areas (for your records) and your invoice. We do not provide legal advice or represent you in disputes.

Is carpet cleaning really included?

Yes — standard move-out carpet extraction is included in the package price. Landlords may still charge for stains, pet damage, or replacement if the carpet is beyond cleaning or already damaged.

What’s the difference between you and a normal move-out cleaner?

We focus on common inspection deductions and we document the finished condition. Most cleaners clean; Deposit Defense is designed to clean and help you protect against avoidable charges.

What exactly is “lease-style” cleaning?

It’s a detailed move-out clean aligned to common landlord checklists: kitchens, bathrooms, floors/edges, detail points, and visible dust areas. If you have a landlord or housing checklist, send it and we’ll use it as a reference.

What add-ons do you offer?

Only three: trash removal, wall hole touch-up (small holes), and odor treatment. Documentation is included in every order.

Are prices final?

The listed package prices are typical rates (+ tax). Final pricing can change based on condition, excessive trash, heavy build-up, severe pet hair/odor, or scope changes. We’ll confirm pricing before work begins.

Do you handle repairs, repainting, or major drywall work?

No. We only do small wall hole touch-up as an add-on. Anything beyond that is outside scope.

Do you remove hazardous waste or biohazards?

No. We do not handle hazardous materials, needles, mold remediation, or biohazard cleanup.
