FAQ

Answers that prevent surprises

Straight talk on what we do, what’s included, and what landlords control.

Do you guarantee I’ll get my full deposit back? +
No. We cannot guarantee a deposit refund or inspection result because landlords control final decisions, and outcomes depend on lease terms and the property’s condition. What we can do is deliver an inspection-focused clean and give you a clear documentation package (photos + invoice) for your records.
Is carpet cleaning included? +
Yes — standard carpet extraction is included in every package. Landlords may still charge for severe staining, damage, pet odor, or replacement decisions. We document visible carpet condition at the time of service.
What exactly is the “documentation package”? +
A photo set of key rooms and common problem areas plus a service invoice for your records. It’s designed to help you show the condition of the home at move-out. It’s included in every order (no extra charge).
What are the only add-ons you offer? +
We keep it simple: trash removal, wall hole touch-up, and odor treatment. Everything else is included in the base move-out clean + carpet + documentation. Add-on pricing depends on scope and is confirmed before work begins.
What if my landlord has a specific checklist? +
Send it by text. We’ll use it as a reference and prioritize those items. If the checklist includes repairs or replacements, note those are landlord decisions and may require other contractors.
Do you do repairs, painting, or heavy junk removal? +
We are a cleaning and documentation service. We do not perform general repairs. The only “repair-style” service we offer is limited wall hole touch-up. We do not handle hazardous waste, biohazards, or large-scale junk hauling.
Are these prices final? What could change the price? +
Prices shown are base packages + tax. Final pricing may change for extreme conditions (heavy pet hair, excessive trash, biohazards, heavy grease, restricted access, or significant extra time). If anything would change the price, we confirm it before starting.
How fast can you schedule? +
Depends on availability and your move-out date. Texting is usually the fastest way to get a quote and a slot. If you’re on a tight PCS timeline, mention it.
Do you work with Fort Sill / PCS move-outs? +
We’re built for PCS timelines and final walkthroughs. If your housing office provides a move-out checklist, send it and we’ll align the clean to it.

Legal & service disclaimer (plain English)

We provide cleaning and documentation. We are not your landlord and we do not control inspection decisions, deposit deductions, or billing. Documentation is for your records and may help support your position, but it is not legal advice.
